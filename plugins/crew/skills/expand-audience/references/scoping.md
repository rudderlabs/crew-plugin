# Scoping a plan into what Crew does

People arrive with a plan that mixes what Crew does with what it does not: contact ingestion, outreach, follow-ups, calendars, posts. Answer each part plainly, from this table, before asking for anything. Hedged answers ("as far as I can see", "probably") were the first thing a test run produced without this table; the user needs a yes, a partly, or a no.

| Ask                                                                                | Crew's answer                                                                                                                                                                                   | What the user hands over                                   |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ingest Gmail, Outlook or calendar contacts, or a LinkedIn connections export       | Not read directly. A list arrives as a CSV; a LinkedIn connections export already is one.                                                                                                       | The CSV (`seeds/people-list.md`, `seeds/accounts-csv.md`)  |
| Dedupe, enrich and score the contacts                                              | Enrichments run on each person once the Outreach Agent has them, and its Observe signals judge fit and intent per person. Crew does not score a contact book as a batch; the list step dedupes. | The ICP: who to look for, in plain words                   |
| Tag by segment (investors, alumni, prospects)                                      | A column in the CSV. It rides on the event and the Agent reads it, so it can shape the message.                                                                                                 | The column, with values that are fine to say to the person |
| Find the right person at each company                                              | Yes: the Expand Agent, with `research_account` and the user's roles. One person per account per decision.                                                                                       | The goal and up to five roles                              |
| Personalised email and LinkedIn messages from role changes, posts and past threads | Yes: the Outreach Agent's Act, built by `linkedin-outreach`. Posts and profile come from enrichments where the workspace has them; past threads from what HeyReach reports.                     | The launch facts and two or three past messages for voice  |
| Approve before anything sends                                                      | Yes: `needsApproval` on every action, with the workspace check in the hand-over step.                                                                                                           | Nothing                                                    |
| Track replies                                                                      | Yes on LinkedIn (HeyReach reports accepts and replies), and on an email channel that reads its mailbox; otherwise no.                                                                           | Nothing                                                    |
| Schedule nudges                                                                    | Crew follow-ups, Agent-wide and off by default; `linkedin-outreach` says when to turn them on.                                                                                                  | Nothing                                                    |
| Propose meeting times, create calendar events                                      | No. A message may offer times in words; booking is another tool.                                                                                                                                | Nothing                                                    |
| LinkedIn or X posts                                                                | No. Crew writes messages to people, not posts.                                                                                                                                                  | Nothing                                                    |
| "In my voice"                                                                      | Writing rules in the Act prompts (`brandVoice` and the message instructions) plus a short sample in the workspace context.                                                                      | Two or three past messages they are happy with             |

What the plan becomes: two Agents and one list. **Expand** finds the person at each account the user does not know anyone at. **Outreach** works every person, found or already known, toward the outcome; its own signals tell a warm contact from a cold one, so they share one Agent with one set of rules rather than two Agents split by warmth (a test run proposed the split; it doubles every later edit). The list splits the same way: accounts to expand, people already known.
